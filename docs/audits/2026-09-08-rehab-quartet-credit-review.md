# Rehab Quartet — credit-review audit

## Conclusion

The presentation supports an initial lender discussion. The linked records do
not yet establish a complete credit-approval package. Missing evidence below
means missing from this package, not a conclusion that it does not exist.

Reviewed the five-page February 2026 4852 Fountain proforma again (narrative,
summary, detailed development budget, multi-year cash flows, dashboard), and

## Material items to resolve

1. **Unit legality and rental support.** The 751 progress-set cover describes
   conversion of an existing two-unit building into three units. A basement
   residence is proposed. The current four-property schedule includes three
   $1,200/month basement residences: $43,200/year of potential rent, about 20.2%
   of total $213,600 gross rent. Approval evidence and rent comparables are not
   linked. Do not treat proposed units as legally approved rental inventory.
2. **Operating cash flow.** The 35% aggregate expense allowance is not a
   tax/insurance/management/maintenance/utilities budget. Replacement reserves
   remain unconfirmed. $127,732.80 is projected NOI, not cash flow available
   after reserves. NOI coverage must not be labeled lender-underwritten DSCR.
3. **Financing denominator.** Base loan is 70% of the $1,258,500 development
   subtotal. Including the financed $55,059.38 reserve, the $936,009.38 loan is
   74.375% of that subtotal, or 71.2575% of $1,313,559.38 identified uses.
   Closing/legal costs remain outside those uses. Display all denominators;
   do not imply the full facility is 70% LTC.
4. **Funding and reconciliation.** Recorded acquisition expenditures are
   $93,061.52 versus $89,000 acquisition budget: $4,061.52 requires allocation.
   This difference is not automatically an overrun; closing charges may explain
   it, but that requires evidence. The $54,000 Fromal member-loan outstanding
   balance and $1,387.20 paid/unmatched management-fee allocation remain open.
   Required equity is a financing calculation, not proof of contributed cash.
5. **Collateral and sponsor evidence.** Current title/lien evidence, subject
   appraisals, sponsor liquidity/financials, guarantor obligations, experience,
   and insurance evidence are not linked. The 736 appraisal concerns a separate
   property and cannot establish the quartet's collateral value.
6. **Construction execution.** A dated, costed construction/draw/lease-up
   schedule is missing. A 12-month interest reserve is not proof that carry
   covers the full execution period. Contracts and stage counts are not a
   verified cost-to-complete or percent-complete certification.
7. **Valuation assumptions.** Presentation cap rate is 7.25%; the older deal
   source stores 8.5%. Owner clarification requested. LTV-only capacity is not
   net refinance proceeds: coverage constraints, expenses, and other payoff
   obligations are not deducted.
8. **751 drawings.** September 7 progress set, nine sheets. A0.0 states two
   existing / three proposed residences, 2,680 SF above grade and 1,340 SF
   basement; these gross areas do not verify 1,165 SF net per unit. The cover
   lists A2.2, A2.3 and A7.0, absent from the supplied PDF. Its drafter designation
   reads “NOT A LICENSED ARCHITECT.” Permit/required professional review remains
   to be evidenced; no jurisdictional conclusion is inferred from that label.

## Deterministic presentation

The page uses fixed UI labels/templates, current structured property records,
and explicit arithmetic. Removed the authored overview paragraph and freeform
reference-document commentary. No inference call or generated development
narrative runs in the rendering path. Unit schedules now use live `unitMix`,
not copies embedded in the financing baseline. The confirmed development
budget remains distinct from actual expenditures. Missing reserves do not
become zero; invalid operating ratios are rejected.

Evidence status requires an explicit typed document link in the deal record
and an actual matching member document. Presence means linked, not approved.
Progress drawings have their own category, separate from approved plans and
permits. The 751 progress file is attached and its observed facts are stored
as structured document metadata with source sheet identifiers.

Deal display name is Rehab Quartet in its canonical note and source records,
including the related Bayard Bundle reference. The stable identifier and
existing URLs remain unchanged so property membership/backlinks still resolve.
